Three Complementary Structures For Capital: Your Local Bank Layered Cash Management Securities Investment Pyramid™

Leppla Capital provides a 3-part money management solution designed to meet your liquidity requirements while increasing your investment returns: 1) Your Bank accepts Deposits and pays Bills. It is your Operating Account. Beyond that, 2) Our Layered Cash Management structure is designed to maximize your short-term yields through investments in U.S. Treasuries and Agencies layered by duration. Thereafter, 3) Our proprietary Securities Investment Pyramid™ is designed to put longer-term investments to work in a manner consistent with your Risk Tolerance and Return Aspirations.

All three structures are linked with immediate Money Transfer capabilities. Money is moved between structures exactly as you wish. This can be scheduled in advance or through an email or phone call — you to us. Either way, Leppla Capital handles the transfers as you instruct. The Goal: Put your funds to work in an effort to meet liquidity requirements and maximize investment returns in a straightforward, efficient manner.

This structure is in use by Labor Union General Funds, Health & Welfare Funds, Apprentice and Training Funds, Construction Firms, Professional Service Firms, Trusts, Endowments, Non-Profits and Individual Investors. In our view: This is classic American small/mid-size business initiative: Innovative, Effective, and Dedicated to Service: all on a Fair Fee Basis.
